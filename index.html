Our
Goal

We strive to guide you in every possible way—not to decide your path for you, but to help you understand it better, prepare for what lies ahead, and move forward with greater confidence and hope.

Because knowing what may come can help you face it with a stronger heart.

Clarity Over Confusion

Busting the
Myths About Astrology

separating belief from misconception — so you can approach the stars with clarity.

01

Myth: Astrology decides your fate.

Reality: Astrology offers insights into possibilities and patterns—it does not take away your free will. Your choices and actions still matter.

02

Myth: Astrology can predict everything exactly.

Reality: Astrology is about understanding tendencies, timing, and possibilities. Life is influenced by circumstances, choices, and actions too.

03

Myth: A difficult prediction means something bad will definitely happen.

Reality: Challenging phases can be understood as periods that require awareness, patience, and preparation. Knowing them in advance can help you navigate them better.

04

Myth: Astrology is only about marriage and career.

Reality: Astrology can offer perspectives on many areas of life—including relationships, career, finances, personal growth, strengths, challenges, and important life phases.

05

Myth: Astrology is meant to create fear.

Reality: At Prarabbdha, we believe astrology should create awareness, not fear—helping you understand yourself, prepare for challenges, and make more conscious choices.

Our Belief

Astrology is not about telling you what you must do.
It is about helping you understand what you may encounter, when you may encounter it, and how you can navigate it with greater awareness.
