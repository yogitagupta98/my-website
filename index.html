Our Goal

At Prarabbdha, our goal is to help you understand the patterns of life and navigate both its gains and challenging phases with greater clarity and resilience. We believe that difficult times are not permanent—time changes, phases pass, and every experience has something to teach us.

Our team of experienced astrologers studies each chart with precision, care, and sincerity, offering thoughtful predictions and personalised consultations. We strive to guide you in every possible way—not to decide your path for you, but to help you understand it better, prepare for what lies ahead, and move forward with greater confidence and hope. Because knowing what may come can help you face it with a stronger heart.

Our Belief

"Astrology is not about telling you what you must do. It is about helping you understand what you may encounter, when you may encounter it, and how you can navigate it with greater awareness."

We steer clear of fear-mongering and fatalism. Every reading is designed to empower you with insight, timing, and conscious choices so you remain the captain of your soul.

Clarity & Truth

Busting the Myths About Astrology

Separating ancient wisdom from common misconceptions.

1

Myth: Astrology decides your fate.

Reality: Astrology offers insights into possibilities and patterns—it does not take away your free will. Your choices and actions still matter.

2

Myth: Astrology can predict everything exactly.

Reality: Astrology is about understanding tendencies, timing, and possibilities. Life is influenced by circumstances, choices, and actions too.

3

Myth: A difficult prediction means something bad will definitely happen.

Reality: Challenging phases can be understood as periods that require awareness, patience, and preparation. Knowing them in advance can help you navigate them better.

4

Myth: Astrology is only about marriage and career.

Reality: Astrology can offer perspectives on many areas of life—including relationships, career, finances, personal growth, strengths, challenges, and important life phases.

5

Myth: Astrology is meant to create fear.

Reality: At Prarabbdha, we believe astrology should create awareness, not fear—helping you understand yourself, prepare for challenges, and make more conscious choices.
